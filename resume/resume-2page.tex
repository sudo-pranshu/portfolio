\section{Academic Projects}
\proj{\lnk{https://github.com/sudo-pranshu/ParkinSense}{ParkinSense} -- Continuous Multi-Modal Wearable Platform for Parkinson's Monitoring \normalfont(nRF52840, BLE, Signal Processing, Python)}
\begin{itemize}
\item Architected a complete end-to-end wearable system (custom firmware, BLE protocol, three parallel real-time signal-processing pipelines, live dashboard) for continuous Parkinson's monitoring, addressing the gap left by single-modality tremor wearables that ignore cardiac and activity context.
\item Built a 104\,Hz sensor-fusion stream on a Seeed XIAO nRF52840 Sense with LSM6DS3 IMU and MAX30102 PPG, sending versioned 208-byte binary BLE packets (10 samples each) with sequence numbers, gap detection and measured device rate; designed BLE protocol v2 for $\sim$100\,Hz PPG.
\item Designed the tremor pipeline: Welch PSD on 4\,s windows every 0.5\,s, 3.5--7.5\,Hz peak search with interpolation, SNR and harmonic rejection, REST/LOW MOTION/ACTIVE motion context and a 2-state HMM; raised sensitivity from 0.69 to 0.99 at 1.00 specificity with 0.024\,Hz median frequency error (synthetic ground truth).
\item Developed a PPG pipeline from scratch (finger detection, signal-quality index, adaptive peak detection, RR intervals) producing heart rate, HRV (RMSSD, SDNN, pNN50) and SpO\textsubscript{2} calibrated against an Amazfit Helio Strap, with SEARCHING, LOCKED, TRACKING and LOST lock states; HR MAE at rest 0.37\,BPM (synthetic).
\item Engineered an accelerometer step counter gated by motion context and a tremor guard, cutting false steps during rest tremor from 23.3 to 0.3/min (synthetic); counted 69 steps against $\approx$86 expected on a real walking recording.
\item Built a FastAPI + Plotly Dash runtime with bit-exact replay, per-recording provenance (79-column capture) and an 84-test CI suite with golden regression on real recordings, enabling offline analysis and future ML dataset generation.
\end{itemize}

\proj{\lnk{https://github.com/rkt-1597/TremoSense-Project}{TremoSense} -- Anti-Tremor Assistive Spoon for Parkinson's Patients \normalfont(Zephyr RTOS, C, EKF, LQR, Simulink)}
\begin{itemize}
\item Engineered a 2-axis active stabilisation system that suppresses pathological hand tremor while preserving intentional motion, improving usability for Parkinson's patients during eating.
\item Integrated the LSM6DS3 6-DoF IMU on a XIAO nRF52840 Sense (ARM Cortex-M4F) sampling at 208\,Hz, driving an MG995 roll and FS90MG pitch servo with commands every 10\,ms through Zephyr threads, a message queue and a semaphore.
\item Designed a 2-state Extended Kalman Filter for roll/pitch estimation, removing gyroscope drift and high-frequency noise, and a 2$\times$4 LQR gain for coordinated two-axis stabilisation with lower control effort than PID.
\item Validated the EKF--LQR design in MATLAB/Simulink at 1000\,Hz: over 90\% roll and 80\% pitch suppression across most of the 3--7\,Hz tremor band; residual RMS 0.346$^\circ$/0.573$^\circ$ under 8$^\circ$, 8\,Hz tremor.
\end{itemize}
